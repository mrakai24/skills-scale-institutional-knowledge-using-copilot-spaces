# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder documents how OctoAcme runs cross-functional delivery work from idea to release. The process starts with initiation, where teams confirm the business need, define success metrics, identify stakeholders, and capture the first one-pager, timeline, and risk inputs needed to decide whether a project should move forward. Once approved, the work moves into planning, where the team turns the initiative into a prioritized backlog, defines acceptance criteria and a shared definition of done, maps milestones and dependencies, and outlines the initial QA approach.

Execution emphasizes small, reviewable increments tracked on a shared project board with regular standups, weekly delivery syncs, and sprint or milestone demos. The core roles stay consistent across the lifecycle: Project Managers coordinate schedules, risks, and communication; Product Managers define outcomes and priorities; Developers build and test the solution; QA partners validate quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide direction, approvals, and feedback. Teams use project documentation, PR descriptions, and status updates as the single source of truth for progress and decision-making.

Risk management and communication continue throughout delivery by maintaining a risk register, reviewing blockers and dependencies in weekly syncs, and escalating issues from the team to PM, Product Lead, and sponsors as needed. Release and deployment require completed acceptance criteria, passing CI and security scans, release notes, rollback planning, and smoke-test verification before and after launch. After each sprint, release, milestone, or incident, the team runs a retrospective, tracks a small set of follow-up actions, and uses those learnings to improve future planning and delivery.
